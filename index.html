          Getting Started
          

            
The glasses open on a home screen. Swipe to highlight Coin Flip or Dice Roll, then tap to select.

            
Flips are a true 50/50 and rolls are a fair six-sided die.

          

        

      


      

        

          Controls
          

            
Use the G2 temple touchpads or the R1 ring.


            

              

                Swipe Up
                Flips the coin, or rolls the die in dice mode.
              


              

                Tap or Swipe Down
                Dismisses the result.
              


              

                Double Tap
                Opens the settings menu (Home and Exit live there). On the home screen, opens the exit prompt.
              

            


            

              Tip: Wait for the result before flipping again.
            

          

        

      


      

        

          Display
          

            
Layout

            
    
              
  • Tally: Top row. Heads left, tails right. In dice mode: roll count and last roll.
    • 
              
  • Coin / Die: Centered on screen.
    • 
              
  • Status: Bottom line. Prompts and results.
    • 
              
  • Background: Unicode drizzle. The pattern shifts during a flip or roll.
    • 
            


            
Drizzle

            
    
              
  • Idle: · °
    • 
              
  • Rising: ↑ ▲ △
    • 
              
  • Falling: ↓ ▼ ▽
    • 
              
  • Rolling: ▕ ◤ ◆ ■

          Settings
          

            
Settings live under the preview. Most are also in the on-glasses menu (double tap).

            
    
              
  • Background pattern: Turns the drizzle on or off.
    • 
              
  • Tally counter: Shows or hides the counts. Counting continues while hidden.
    • 
              
  • Reset tally on startup: Zeroes the counts each time the app opens.
    • 
              
  • Mode: Coin Flip or Dice Roll. On the glasses, pick from the home screen.
    • 
              
  • Reset tally: Clears the counts now.
    • 
            

            
Settings and the tally persist between sessions.
