          Getting Started
          

            
Swipe up to flip. Results are 50/50.

          

        

      


      

        

          Controls
          

            
Use the G2 temple touchpads or the R1 ring.


            

              

                Swipe Up
                Flips the coin.
              


              

                Double Tap
                Opens the exit prompt.
              

            


            

              Tip: Wait for the result before flipping again.
            

          

        

      


      

        

          Display
          

            
Layout

            
    
              
  • Tally: Top row. Heads left, tails right.
    • 
              
  • Coin: Centered on screen.
    • 
              
  • Status: Bottom line. Prompts and results.
    • 
              
  • Background: Unicode drizzle. The palette shifts during a flip.
    • 
            


            
Drizzle

            
    
              
  • Idle: · °
    • 
              
  • Rising: ↑ ▲ △
    • 
              
  • Falling: ↓ ▼ ▽
    • 
            

          

        

      


      

        

          Settings
          

            
Toggles live above, under the preview.

            
    
              
  • Background pattern: Turns the drizzle on or off.
    • 
              
  • Tally counter: Shows or hides the heads/tails count. Off by default; the count keeps tracking while hidden.
    • 
              
  • Reset tally on startup: Zeroes the count each time the app opens.
    • 
            

            
Settings and the tally persist between sessions. Reset tally clears the count now.
